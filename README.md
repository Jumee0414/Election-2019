2019 Nigerian General Election Analysis Dashboard Report

The 2019 Nigerian General Election saw a total of 82 million registered voters and 29 million accredited voters. The analysis focuses on the race between the two main parties: the APC (All Progressives Congress), which won with 15 million votes, and the PDP (People's Democratic Party), which came in as the first runner-up with 11 million votes. The state-by-state breakdown reveals strong regional differences in party support, with APC securing a slightly higher number of states (19 vs. 18 for PDP).

 Key Findings
1. Overall Election Metrics
The top-line figures establish the scale and turnout of the election:

Metric	Value	
Total number of reg. voters	82M	The total electorate size.
Accredited Voters	29M	The actual number of voters who participated.
APC (Winner)	15M	The total votes secured by the winning party.
PDP (First Runner Up)	11M	The total votes secured by the main opposition.
Total for others	870K	Votes secured by all other minor parties.
The gap between the winner (APC) and the first runner-up (PDP) is 4 million votes.
